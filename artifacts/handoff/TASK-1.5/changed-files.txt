## Product code — packages/paper/paper-foundation/src/ir/

A   packages/paper/paper-foundation/src/ir/problem-contract.ts
      New canonical kinds DataArtifact / RequirementSpec / SymbolSpec with
      closed enums and a strict sha256 content_hash; the cross-cutting
      semantic guards (raw-source <-> requirement-source consistency, symbol
      role binding, run-input role binding, figure union, same-scope token
      uniqueness); and `minimumProblemContractSatisfied`.

M   packages/paper/paper-foundation/src/ir/schema.ts
      ProblemSpec reduced to `requirement_refs[]` (nested subproblems /
      required_outputs / constraints removed); ModelSpec reduced to
      `variable_refs[]` / `parameter_refs[{symbol_ref,value}]` (embedded
      meaning/unit removed); RunArtifact `input_refs` -> `input_data_refs[]`;
      IR_KINDS / IR_SCHEMAS / ID_FIELD_BY_KIND / IrObjectMap extended to the
      three new kinds.

M   packages/paper/paper-foundation/src/ir/refs.ts
      Reference table gained RequirementSpec.source_data_ref and
      SymbolSpec.scope_ref. FigureSpec.data_refs deliberately widened to
      'ANY': the table can name one target per field, and the legal target is
      the union Result | DataArtifact, which is enforced by the contract
      guard instead (C-015).

M   packages/paper/paper-foundation/src/ir/bridge.ts
      `IrBridgeDecision` gained contractFailures / contract / contractSatisfied;
      `inspectProblemContract` walks the store for per-element and
      cross-cutting failures and builds the bound minimum-contract summary;
      FORMAL and FAST require `contractSatisfied`. Orphan ModelSpecs are
      validated explicitly (RT-B-01).

M   packages/paper/paper-foundation/src/ir/parse.ts
      `scanIrValue` walks under a shared node budget (MAX_IR_VALUE_NODES) and
      gained the `too_large` verdict, so the typed ingress carries the same
      size bound as the text ingress (RT-A-02).

M   packages/paper/paper-foundation/src/ir/index.ts
      Public surface: the three new schemas and their closed enums, the guard
      functions, the new parse constant, and `modelSpecSchema` (which had been
      dropped from the barrel).
